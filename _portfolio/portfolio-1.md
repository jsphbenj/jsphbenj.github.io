The Sprig on Rosemary is a mixed-use development project designed as for Site Planning and Urban Design coursework at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill.
The project site is a 0.5-acre parcel located at the intersection of Rosemary Street and Columbia Street, 
and aims to create a vibrant and sustainable community that integrates residential, commercial, and public spaces.
This project was created with a team of five. My role in the project was SketchUp drawings and GIS analyses for the site analysis.

Skills: Urban Design, Site Planning & Analysis, SketchUp
